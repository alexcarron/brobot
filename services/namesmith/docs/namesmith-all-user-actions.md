# Namesmith All User Actions

A reference of every action a player can take.

## Earning Tokens

### Mine Tokens
Earn a small amount of tokens repeatedly with no cooldown or limit.

### Claim Refill
Earn a larger amount of tokens once per cooldown period.

### Toggle Refill Reminders
Turn on or off a reminder that tells you when your next refill is ready.

## Getting Characters

### Buy Mystery Box
Spend tokens to open a mystery box of a chosen type and gain a random character from it, one or many at a time.

### Craft Characters
Turn characters you own into new ones using a recipe.

## Trading

### Initiate Trade
Offer another player an exchange of your characters for theirs.

### Accept Trade
Agree to a trade offered to you and complete the exchange.

### Modify Trade
Change which characters are exchanged and send the offer back to the other player.

### Decline Trade
Reject a trade offered to you.

## Building Your Name

### Rearrange Name
Set your current name from the characters you own.

### Publish Name
Lock your current name into your next published name slot.

## Roles and Perks

### Choose a Role
Choose one role at the start of the game.

### Pick a Perk
Choose one perk during a perk window.

## Quests

### Complete Quest
Claim a quest's reward once you meet its requirements.

## Voting

### Vote for a Name
Rank a published name as one of your top three favorites.

### Undo a Vote
Remove a rank you gave to a name.

### See My Votes
Review your current ranked votes.

### Clear My Votes
Remove all of your ranked votes at once.

## Checking Information

### See Inventory
View your own or another player's characters.

### See Current Name
View your own or another player's current name.

### See Tokens
View your own or another player's token balance.

### See Perks
View your own or another player's perks.

### See Role
View your own or another player's role.
