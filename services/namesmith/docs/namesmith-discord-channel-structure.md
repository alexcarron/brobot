# Namesmith Discord Server Text Channel Structure

## [No Category] (Top of Server)
- `#verify-you-are-human`: Contains a simple captcha users that first join the server to gain access to the server. Unverified users can only see this channel. Verified users cannot see this channel, but get access to all other channels.
- `#development-news`: I announce things related to the development of Namesmith and where Brobot announces the start of the game with its theme
- `#rules-and-info`: I send the rules and gameplay mechanics of the game, exactly copying what is written in `namesmith-rules-and-gameplay-mechanics.md`

## See Names
- `#the-results`: Brobot sends the placements of every published name. Hidden until voting ends
- `#names-to-vote-on`: Brobot sends all the published names to vote on, and players can vote on them with buttons. Hidden until voting starts. Hidden again once voting ends.
- `#published-names`: Brobot announces when a player publishes their name here. Hidden once voting starts.
- `#name-archive`: Brobot sends every archived namesmith game at the end of the game including the game version, theme, player usernames, their names, and their placements

## Roles, Perks, and Quests
- `#choose-a-role`: Brobot sends all the roles, their names, descriptions, and perks at the start of the game. Players can choose their role with buttons.
- `#pick-a-perk`: Empty at start of game. Brobot sends new perks including their names and descriptions twice a week during the name smithing phase, replacing any existing messages. Players can pick a perk to get with buttons.
- `#daily-quests`: Brobot sends the new daily quests every day including their names, descriptions, and rewards. Players can attempt to complete them with buttons.
- `#weekly-quests`: Brobot sends the new weekly quests every week including their names, descriptions, and rewards. Players can attempt to complete them with buttons.
- `#hidden-quests`: Brobot sends the new hidden quests every day including their names, descriptions, and rewards. Players can attempt to complete them with buttons. Hidden to all players every day. Shown to players that complete all daily quests.

## Talk
- `#general`: Players send messages to talk about anything
- `#give-feedback`: Players give feedback on Namesmith and suggestions on what to add or how to improve it
- `#report-a-bug`: Players report bugs or errors they run in to

## Earn Tokens
- `#mine-tokens`: Players run `/mine-tokens` to mine tokens
- `#claim-refill`: Players run `/claim-refill` to claim their refills
- `#sell-characters`: Players run `/sell-characters` to sell unwanted characters for tokens

## Get Characters
- `#buy-mystery-boxes`: Players run `/buy-mystery-box` to buy mystery boxes
- `#trade-characters`: Brobot sends trade request messages and trade response messages. Players run `/trade` to request trading characters with other players
- `#craft-characters`: Players run `/craft-characters` to craft characters
