# Namesmith Publishing Name Mechanic
A command used to lock in your current name as a permanent, anonymous entry for voting, at the cost of the characters it's made of
# Slots and Costs
Every player has up to 4 published name slots, filled in order starting from the lowest empty one
- 1st slot: free
- 2nd slot: 250 tokens
- 3rd slot: 500 tokens
- 4th slot: 1000 tokens
- Re-filling a slot that was already paid for is free again
# Consuming Your Current Name
Publishing removes the published name's characters from your inventory and empties your current name
- Only the exact characters used in the published name are removed from your inventory
- Your current name becomes fully empty afterward
- This applies both when you publish with `/publish-name` and when a name is auto-published for you at the end of the build phase if you never published one yourself
# Possible Responses
## Confirmation
Are you sure you want to publish your current name, **Namey**, as your 2nd published name?
-# This will remove those characters from your inventory and leave your current name empty.
This will cost you **250 tokens**.
-# You have 2 available published name slots remaining.
-# You have 500 tokens.
## Success
Your current name was published as your 2nd published name:
> Namey
-# Its characters were removed from your inventory. Your current name is now empty.
-# This published name cost 250 tokens.
-# You now have 250 tokens.
## Failure
You have no current name to publish.
